The Strategy

The Short-Term-Holder (STH) cost basis is the average price paid for every bitcoin that changed hands in roughly the last five months — the break-even line of the market's most price-sensitive participants. Bull markets hold above it, bear markets live below it, and capitulation happens well beneath it. Computed from blockchain transaction data, it reflects real capital at risk, not sentiment or chart patterns.

The strategy reads this line every day and keeps the portfolio in one of three states: all-in Bitcoin when the market capitulates far below the cost basis or when an up-trend is confirmed above it; cash (earning stable-coin yield) when the trend breaks. A second on-chain series — the unrealised profit of long-term holders — marks the euphoric extremes where the position is scaled out in slices and later partly re-bought.

Why it is different:

  • Decisive, not incremental — full exposure in the regimes that produce the returns, cash in the ones that destroy it
  • Sells only on weakness: every scale-out slice waits for short-term momentum to turn
  • A no-loss rule means a slice is never sold below the price it was last bought at
  • One decision per day, executed automatically in a sub-account in your own name

1 · On-Chain Regime Signal

The signal layer

The STH cost basis separates Bitcoin's bull and bear regimes cleanly: price above it means recent buyers are in profit and dips get bought; price below it means they are under water and rallies get sold. Because it is built from on-chain transactions it reacts within weeks, not months.

A second series — the profit position of long-term holders — identifies the cycle extremes where distribution historically begins.

Advantages
  • Has distinguished bull and bear regimes since 2012
  • Fast enough to catch capitulation lows within days
  • Independent of price-only technicals and sentiment
  • One decision per day — low noise, low churn

2 · Decisive Allocation

The position engine

Rather than drip-feeding a fixed amount regardless of conditions, the strategy is binary by design: it deploys all available cash when price collapses to a capitulation discount or when an up-trend is confirmed, and returns to cash when the trend breaks.

Idle cash is not wasted — it is swept into a yield-bearing stable-coin and converted back just-in-time for the next buy.

Advantages
  • Full exposure in the regimes that produce the returns
  • Sidesteps the deepest part of each bear market
  • Cash on hand exactly when capitulation buys appear
  • Entries are rule-driven, never date-driven

3 · Scale-Out & Re-Buy

The distribution engine

Cycle tops are processes, not moments. As long-term holders' profit climbs through progressively more extreme levels, the Bitcoin share is stepped down in three slices and a final exit — each sold only once short-term momentum has turned, never into strength.

After a meaningful pull-back the position is rebuilt, re-arming the ladder for the next leg. The no-loss rule stops the ladder churning capital in a sideways market.

Advantages
  • Never all-in at a cycle top — profit banked in stages
  • Raises cash on weakness, redeploys it lower
  • Final rung exits completely at the blow-off extreme
  • Beat single all-out exits and un-gated ladders in testing

Guard-Rails That Stop Bad Trades
Momentum confirmation

No exit or scale-out fires on a single bad close — short-term momentum must have turned first, so the strategy sells weakness, not strength.

Minimum holding period

Fresh entries are protected from same-week whipsaws; the on-chain line is allowed to do its work before an exit is considered.

Trailing protection

A trailing stop below the post-entry peak caps the damage if a confirmed trend reverses violently.

No-loss ladder & macro filter

Partial sales are never made below the last purchase price, and no new trend entry is opened at a cycle-top extreme.

What This Strategy Is Not
Not a guarantee

Every rule parameter was chosen on historical data. Fitted on one half of history and tested on the other, the strategy beat holding by roughly 1.5–2× — not the 50× of the full-history back-test (page 1). The future can differ from every past cycle.

Not always ahead of holding

In strongly trending years with shallow pull-backs the strategy's exits cost it ground: in the back-test it lagged simple holding in . It is designed to win across a full cycle, not every year.

Not low-volatility

When fully invested it carries Bitcoin's full daily volatility. The back-test's worst peak-to-trough fall was ; a client who joins just before a trend break will experience a loss first.

Not data-independent

Signals depend on third-party on-chain series. A different provider, a methodology change or an outage would change behaviour; BitWealth monitors data freshness daily and pauses trading when it is stale.

Bitcoin & platform risk

Bitcoin is a high-risk asset. Assets are held at VALR, a regulated South African exchange; exchange insolvency, regulatory change and stable-coin issuer risk are outside BitWealth's control.

Research results, not live results

The strategy has been researched and back-tested with next-day fills and the full fee stack; it has not yet produced a live track record. Live results will differ from back-tests.

Frequently Asked Questions
How much do I need to start, and can I contribute monthly?
Both a lump sum and a monthly debit order (in Rand) are supported, in any combination. Contributions are invested immediately while the strategy is in Bitcoin and held as yielding cash while it is not.
Who holds my Bitcoin?
You do — in a dedicated sub-account at VALR opened in your name. BitWealth operates it through a trade-only API key that cannot withdraw. You can view balances directly at the exchange at any time.
What happens to my money when the strategy is "in cash"?
It is swept into a yield-bearing US-dollar stable-coin and converted back the moment a buy signal fires. Yield is variable and not guaranteed; the back-test assumes roughly 10% p.a.
How are fees charged?
1% p.a. management on portfolio value, deducted monthly, plus 10% of new profit above the previous high-water mark. There is no fee on deposits or withdrawals beyond the exchange's own costs. All figures in this document are shown after these fees.
Can I withdraw at any time?
Yes. Withdrawals are processed from your sub-account in Rand; there is no lock-in and no exit penalty.
Why doesn't the document show the exact trigger levels?
The thresholds, slice sizes and confirmation rules are BitWealth's intellectual property. What we do publish is every trade the rules would have produced: the interactive back-tester at bitwealth.co.za replays them for any dates and deposits you choose.
How does this differ from simply holding Bitcoin?
Holding captures everything — including every 50–80% drawdown. The strategy aims to capture most of the up-cycles while spending the worst of the down-cycles in cash. Out-of-sample this produced roughly 1.5–2× the value of holding at well under half the drawdown; in some single years holding still did better.
What do "in-sample" and "out-of-sample" mean, and why show both?
In-sample: the rules were tuned on the full history and then measured on it — it shows the mechanics but flatters the result. Out-of-sample: the rules were tuned on 2015–2019 only and then run blind on 2020–2026 (and vice versa) — the closest a back-test can get to a fair trial. We show both because the gap between them is itself the most useful piece of information: it is the share of the headline that came from hindsight.
