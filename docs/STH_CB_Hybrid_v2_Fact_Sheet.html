STH Cost Basis Hybrid Strategy v2

All-in at capitulation · scale out into euphoria · re-buy the pull-back — driven by on-chain holder cost bases
Prepared by BitWealth · Confidential — For Review Only
The strategy in one paragraph

The Short-Term-Holder (STH) cost basis is the average price paid for every bitcoin that moved on-chain in the last 155 days — the break-even line of the market's most price-sensitive participants. Bull markets hold above it, bear markets live below it, and capitulation happens 25–35 % beneath it. Version 1 of this strategy bought everything at 75 % of the STH cost basis (or at the Long-Term-Holder PVR −0.5σ band), rode established up-trends from 12 % above the line and sold everything in one go at euphoria. Version 2 keeps every one of those rules and changes only the top: instead of a single all-out it sells in four slices as price climbs through the LTH PVR +1σ, +1.5σ, +2σ and +2.5σ bands — each slice only once short-term momentum has turned negative — and buys the slices back after a 10 % pull-back (or when price returns to within 10 % of the cost basis). Idle cash earns stable-coin yield. The result: the same drawdown, and more money.

New in v2 — the scale-out ladder and the dip re-buy

SCALE OUT R15 · Ladder

  • Inside a hold, each time price closes above the next LTH PVR upper band the BTC share steps down: +1σ → 75 %, +1.5σ → 50 %, +2σ → 25 %, +2.5σ → 0 %
  • Rungs latch — a dip back below a band does not undo the slice
  • A hold entered above a rung starts at that rung; nothing is sold on day one

GATE Momentum gate

  • A rung is armed when price crosses the band, but the slice is only sold once the 2-day rate of change is ≤ −2 % — the strategy sells the first sign of weakness at height, never into strength
  • Applies to every rung, including the final 0 % exit
  • Without this gate a ladder under-performs the single all-out — tested, not assumed

RE-BUY R16 · Dip re-buy

  • After a slice, go back to 100 % BTC when price is 10 % below its post-slice peak or back at ≤ 110 % of the STH cost basis
  • Re-arms the ladder so the same rungs can sell again on the next leg up; at most 3 re-buys per hold
  • 5-day cooldown between any slice and re-buy (either direction) — no next-day flip-flops. Full exits ignore the cooldown

Exit rules — when bitcoin becomes cash

SELL R2 · Capitulation exit

  • From a capitulation hold, price recovers to 95 % of the cost basis; sell only once 2-day momentum ≤ −2 %
  • Fired: Jul-18 $8.2k · Jan-20 $8.1k · Aug-21 $45.4k · Apr-22 $46.4k · Nov-22 $19.4k · Oct-23 $26.8k · May-26 $80.5k

SELL R3 / R12 · Trend exit

  • R3 price ≤ 97 % of the cost basis, momentum ≤ −2 % and the cost basis flat or falling over 21 days · R12 trailing stop 35 % below the peak since entry
  • Fired: Sep-19 $8.1k (stop) · Mar-20 $7.95k · Sep-23 $25.8k · Aug-24 $59.9k · Mar-25 $88.9k · Oct-25 $109.5k

SELL R15 · Final rung (+2.5σ)

  • The last rung of the ladder is the v1 euphoria exit: price ≥ the LTH PVR +2.5σ band, momentum turned → sell everything. Replaces v1's "2× cost basis" rule
  • Fired: Dec-17 $16.6k · Apr-21 $61.4k · Oct-21 $61.1k
Filters — what stops bad trades

FILTER Minimum hold

  • No exit rule may fire within 15 days of an entry — removes same-week whipsaws (ladder slices are exempt)

FILTER Post-exit wait

  • After a final-rung exit, no trend re-entry until price has come back to 85 % of the cost basis (v1: 90 %) — sits out the top-formation chop

FILTER Macro top

  • No trend entries while price is above the LTH PVR +2.5σ band; capitulation buys are never blocked
Why these two on-chain lines

STH cost basis — the regime line

  • Average acquisition price of coins younger than 155 days (Research Bitcoin realized_price_sth); reacts within weeks and has separated bull from bear since 2012

LTH PVR bands — the cycle extremes

  • BitWealth's Long-Term-Holder Profit-to-Volatility model. −0.5σ = long-term holders near break-even (accumulate); +1σ…+2.5σ = statistically extreme unrealised profit (distribute in steps)

Why this strategy — the rationale in three lines
  • Buy fear, measured. Capital goes in only when price is ≥ 25 % below what recent buyers paid (STH cost basis) or long-term holders are near break-even (LTH PVR −0.5σ) — the conditions at every major bottom since 2017
  • Ride the trend, filter the noise. An up-trend ≥ 12 % above the line is held through ordinary pull-backs: an exit needs price below the line, negative momentum and a flattening cost basis, after a 15-day minimum hold
  • Sell greed, in steps. Through LTH PVR +1σ … +2.5σ the BTC share is stepped down — each slice only once momentum turns — and partly re-bought on the pull-back, so the strategy is never all-in at a cycle top
