Our Approach

Bitcoin rewards patience and punishes emotion. We replace both with data and rules.

On-Chain Intelligence

We read the blockchain itself — what recent buyers actually paid and how much profit long-term holders are sitting on — to tell whether Bitcoin is in capitulation, trend or euphoria.

Rules, Not Opinions

Every buy and sell follows a written rule set that has been back-tested across multiple Bitcoin cycles. No forecasts, no discretion, no gut feel — one decision per day.

Buy Fear, Sell Greed

All-in when the market capitulates, fully invested while the up-trend is confirmed, and scaling out in slices — only on weakness — as the cycle reaches euphoric extremes.

Built-In Guard-Rails

Momentum confirmation, minimum holding periods, trailing protection and a no-loss rule on partial sales filter out the whipsaws that erode returns.

Your Own Exchange Account

Assets are held in a dedicated sub-account in your name at VALR, a regulated South African exchange. BitWealth trades it; it never pools or re-hypothecates your coins.

Radical Transparency

A client portal with every order, fee and balance, monthly statements, and a public back-tester so you can verify the strategy before you commit a cent.

About BitWealth

BitWealth was founded on a simple observation: most people who buy Bitcoin lose to the asset itself — they buy late, sell early and get shaken out at the bottom. The data to avoid that is public, on the blockchain. The discipline to act on it, every day, is not.

We build that discipline into software. Our strategies are researched, back-tested across full market cycles and published with their stress tests — then executed automatically in your own exchange account.

  • Research first. Every rule is tested out-of-sample and under moved assumptions before it touches client capital.
  • Honest reporting. We publish the out-of-sample result next to the full-history back-test, and the years the strategy lost to simple holding — not just the headline multiple.
  • South African roots, global asset. Rand funding, local support, Bitcoin exposure.
