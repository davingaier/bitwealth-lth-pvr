116: 8.	AUM MILESTONE EARN-IN (ADDITIONAL 30%)
117: 8.1	Simon may earn up to a further 30% equity in three equal tranches of 10% (100 Ordinary Shares per tranche), each constituting a transfer of existing shares from Davin. No new shares are issued. The tranches and Milestones are:
119: 8.2	AUM means the aggregate Rand value of all client portfolios managed by the Company at month-end, as reported in the BitWealth platform NAV report and confirmed by the Company’s accounting records.
120: 8.3	Milestones are sequential: a later tranche cannot vest before the earlier tranche is earned or resolved under clause 9.
121: 8.4	TOTAL-AUM BASIS. Simon’s milestone vesting is tied to TOTAL Company AUM, including clients introduced by Davin, third parties or otherwise. The Introduced Client Register is maintained for revenue-share and record purposes only and does not limit milestone vesting.
122: 8.5	OBJECTIVE VERIFICATION. A Milestone is achieved when total AUM meets the target at a month-end AND remains at or above it to the following month-end (two consecutive month-ends), verified from platform, custodian, exchange and accounting records.
123: 8.6	ADMINISTRATIVE CONFIRMATION. Board confirmation of Milestone achievement is administrative only and may not be unreasonably withheld or delayed. If the evidence shows the threshold met and sustained and the Board has not confirmed within 10 Business Days, the Milestone is deemed achieved.
124: 8.7	Once a Milestone is confirmed or deemed achieved, Davin shall effect the relevant share transfer within 5 Business Days in the same manner as clause 7.5.
126: 9.	MILESTONE SHORTFALL — NEAR-MISS VESTING
127: 9.1	This clause applies equally to all three Milestone Tranches.
128: 9.2	STEP 1 — EXTENSION ELIGIBILITY (measured at the Milestone Deadline): if total AUM is at least 80% of the relevant target, Simon automatically receives a single 6-month extension for that tranche. If AUM is below 80% at the Milestone Deadline, the tranche lapses immediately and no extension is granted.
129: 9.3	STEP 2 — FULL VESTING: if AUM reaches 100% of the target at any point during the 6-month extension, the full 10% tranche vests and is transferred to Simon within 5 Business Days.
130: 9.4	STEP 3 — PRO-RATA VESTING: if, at the end of the 6-month extension, AUM is at least 80% but below 100% of the target, Simon vests a pro-rata portion = (AUM at extension end ÷ Milestone target) × 10%. Example: R45m of R50m target (90%) → Simon vests 9%; the residual 1% remains with Davin.
131: 9.5	STEP 4 — LAPSE: if, at the end of the extension, AUM has fallen below 80% of the target, the tranche lapses in full and the shares remain with Davin. Simon retains all previously vested tranches and his Founding Shares.
132: 9.6	If a Milestone Deadline is extended under clause 9.2, the deadlines of all subsequent tranches are extended by the same period, because tranches must be achieved in sequence.

151: 12.2.1	death or permanent total disability;
152: 12.2.2	retirement at or after age 65;

161: 12.4.2	wilful misconduct or gross negligence causing material harm to the Company; or
162: 12.4.3	a final (non-appealable) regulatory finding against that Party in connection with the Company’s business.
163: 12.5	BAD LEAVER CONSEQUENCE. For the Bad Leaver:
164: 12.5.1	any unearned/unvested equity lapses immediately;
165: 12.5.2	EARNED shares are acquired by the Company or the remaining Shareholder at FAIR MARKET VALUE, EXCEPT where the trigger is fraud, theft or dishonesty, in which case earned shares are acquired at nominal par value of R1.00 per share;
166: 12.5.3	any forced transfer requires PRIOR INDEPENDENT DETERMINATION by a qualified auditor, expert or arbitrator; and
167: 12.5.4	NEITHER Party may unilaterally declare the other a Bad Leaver. A declaration is of no effect until confirmed by independent determination or arbitration.

181: 14.	NEW VENTURES AND INNOVATION REVENUE
182: 14.1	DEFAULT — NEWCO. Any materially new product, business line or revenue stream originated by Simon and separable from the core AUM business shall be developed in a NewCo with a fresh shareholding structure.

185: 14.4	EXCEPTION — INSIDE BITWEALTH. Where ALL of the following apply, a Simon-originated non-AUM revenue stream may be kept inside BitWealth:
186: 14.4.1	Simon personally originates a genuinely new, NON-AUM revenue stream (not performance, platform, management or exchange fees on managed assets);
187: 14.4.2	the Board decides to keep it inside BitWealth; and
188: 14.4.3	Simon actively leads the commercial and business-development delivery of that stream (concept origination, client/partner relationships, go-to-market, sales and revenue growth), but is NOT required to perform IT development, engineering, product build or technical operations.
189: 14.5	INNOVATION REVENUE SHARE. Where clause 14.4 applies, Simon receives a ring-fenced Innovation Revenue Share of 10% of the NET revenue of that specific stream only, for a period of 48 months from first revenue, payable only while Simon actively leads the stream, with the stream separately accounted and auditable.
190: 14.6	RING-FENCE. The Innovation Revenue Share applies ONLY to the specific new non-AUM stream. It NEVER touches AUM-based revenue (performance, platform, management or exchange fees) from any client.
191: 14.7	STACKING. The Innovation Revenue Share stacks on Simon’s equity dividend: Simon first receives 10% of net stream revenue, then his equity % of the residual 90%. The effective formula is: Simon’s total = 10% + (equity% × 90%).
192: 14.8	RECIPROCITY. The same framework applies to Davin-originated ventures. Simon has no Innovation Revenue Share claim over a stream he did not originate and does not actively lead.
193: 14.9	ANTI-CIRCUMVENTION. Neither Party may re-label core AUM revenue as a ‘new stream’ to avoid the other’s legitimate share. Characterisation disputes go to independent expert determination.
194: 14.10	IP IN NEW STREAMS. All IP created in connection with any new revenue stream or NewCo vests in the Company (or NewCo), not in Simon or Davin personally.
